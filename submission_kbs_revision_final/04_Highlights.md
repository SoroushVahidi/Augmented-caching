# Highlights

Manuscript: KNOSYS-D-26-07461R1
Title: Decision-aligned eviction-value prediction for learning-augmented caching

---

- Finite-horizon eviction-value target for candidate-level cache decisions.
- Online replay across seven trace families at cache sizes 32, 64, and 128.
- Matched comparison finds no advantage over seven classical and learned baselines.
- Eviction-loss is worst among four tested finite-horizon objectives.
- Target degeneracy and horizon truncation explain the negative result.
- Controlled timing quantifies substantial learned-policy overhead.
