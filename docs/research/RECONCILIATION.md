# November research report and later evidence

The [November report](../../post-acceptance-research/README.md) preserves its original scientific scope and findings. It covers the earlier stateless and recurrent runs through Phase 2C-B. Its original Git revision remains available in [draft PR #53](https://github.com/SoroushVahidi/Augmented-caching/pull/53). The integration checksum manifest verifies the original README bytes at their archived snapshot path. The live README can evolve independently.

Subsequent A/B/C and E/F campaigns produced additional retrospective replays and admission diagnostics. The G and I/J/M validation campaigns tested temporal modeling, forecasting and supervision changes. Their validation findings do not establish a complete outer portfolio or independent confirmation. Phase 2C-B configurations with names beginning with D are separate from the later deferred Experiment D. See the [experiment index](README.md) for evidence scope and the [methodology](METHODOLOGY.md) for comparison rules.

Claims about later native implementation feasibility require parity, matched timing against both LRU implementations, and the corresponding completed portfolio evidence. The November report remains the record of its execution dates and timing configuration.
