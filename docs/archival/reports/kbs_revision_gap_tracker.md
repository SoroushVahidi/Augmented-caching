## Open methodological caveat (training stage)

`scripts/train_evict_value_wulver_v1.py:_load_rows_from_manifest` shuffles shard
paths with a fixed seed and stops reading shards once it has buffered
`max_rows * 2` raw rows (here `--max-val-rows 250000` → stops at 500k raw rows),
then randomly samples down to `max_rows`. Because this is an early-stop over a
shuffled shard *prefix*, not a proportional draw across all shards, the
validation-set metrics used for model/horizon selection in this run cover only
5 of 7 trace families (**brightkite, cloudphysics, metacdn, twemcache,
wiki2018** — citibike and metakv are entirely absent), using only ~4,572 of
~141,000 available validation decisions (~3.2%). This pattern is identical
across all three horizons and all three models (same seed, same shard order),
so it is a deterministic sampling artifact, not corruption — citibike and
metakv data exist and are populated correctly in the full dataset
(`dataset_summary_extended_heavy_r1.json` confirms nonzero val rows for both).
It does mean the `best_overall` choice (horizon=4, random_forest) was selected
on a non-representative subset of trace families, and the close margin between
random_forest (0.02078) and hist_gb (0.02100) at horizon=4 could plausibly flip
under full-family validation coverage. Not blocking, but worth disclosing if
this selection is cited in the manuscript.
